Questions 💭
Frequently asked.
everything you've been wondering ✦
How do I purchase a course?+
Simply click "Purchase" on any course, and you'll be connected to our team via WhatsApp. Share your details, confirm payment, and you're enrolled — usually within the hour.
Can I download the PDF materials?+
PDFs are provided in a secure viewer only. Downloading, printing, and copying are disabled to protect teacher content. Every page is watermarked with your name.
How do consultation sessions work?+
Fill in the booking form with your subject, preferred date, and time. Your request goes to the admin team who will confirm via WhatsApp within 24 hours.
Is there a free trial?+
Some introductory lessons are available for free. Message us on WhatsApp to find out what's currently available for your subject area.
Which subjects are available?+
We cover Maths, Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), English, History, Economics, and Computer Science. More subjects are added regularly.
